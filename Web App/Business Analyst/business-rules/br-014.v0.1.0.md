# BR-014

Any third-party service or dependency integrated with notes_api must contractually and technically store/process data only within the EU

**Rationale:** Closes residency-chain gaps that could fail compliance audits
**Enforcement:** Vendor review during integration + residency audit report
**Exception:** None
