# US-004-04: verify that any third-party services or dependencies used by notes_api also store/process data within the EU

## Definition

As a **compliance auditor**, I want to **verify that any third-party services or dependencies used by notes_api also store/process data within the EU**, so that **closes gaps in the residency chain that could fail the compliance KPI**.

**Epic:** [[EP-004]]

## Acceptance Criteria
- [[AC-004-04-01]] — Given notes_api depends on a third-party service, when the auditor reviews the service's data processing location, then the location is documented as EU-resident
- [[AC-004-04-02]] — Given a third-party service's data residency cannot be confirmed, when the audit is run, then the dependency is flagged for remediation before launch
