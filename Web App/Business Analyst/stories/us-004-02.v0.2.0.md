As a **compliance auditor**, I want to **retrieve a report listing the region of every data store and service used by notes_api**, so that **enables pre-launch audits to confirm 100% EU-only data storage**.

**Epic:** [[EP-004]]

## Acceptance Criteria
- [[AC-004-02-01]] — Given notes_api infrastructure is deployed, when the auditor requests the residency report, then the report lists each data store/service with its region and confirms all are EU
- [[AC-004-02-02]] — Given a service's region is undetermined, when the report is generated, then that service is flagged as unverified rather than assumed compliant
